Folks,

`draft-chapman-a2a-mls` continues to specify Governed Objects together with MLS
session mechanics for agent messaging.

Separately, we have published a **GO-only** A2A extension profile (no MLS in
the extension MUSTs) under
`https://atom.qwixl.dev/a2a/data-object/v1`, with a SPEC and conformance
corpus in the reference implementation repository. The draft's Extension
Declaration section now clarifies that MLS-only messages must not stamp the
GO URI.

Review ask remains narrow: the credential-binding construction in the draft.

Spec pointer: https://github.com/Qwixl/Atom/tree/main/spec/extensions/data-object-v1
